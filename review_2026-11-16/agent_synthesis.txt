AGENT LITERATURE SYNTHESIS
Cutoff and verification date: 2026-11-16
Coverage: exactly 50 unique publications, A01–A50. Their JSON annotations distinguish source findings from our transfer proposals and limitations. Most entries are abstract-level reviews; closest novelty threats received targeted full-text inspection. No experiments were reproduced. This is a purposefully selected review, not an exhaustive systematic search.

MANUSCRIPT UNDERSTANDING
The historical 40-episode experiment is primarily an evaluator audit. The nominal unavailable condition was not a demonstrated impossibility condition: an unconditional acceptance veto rejected candidates that could satisfy the raw finite suite. Missing action receipts and controller-induced stops prevent strong behavioral conclusions. The later continuation cohorts are exploratory, procedurally heterogeneous, and include regressions even under truthful PASS. Security failure and legitimate-function denial must be separated. The manuscript's most promising future contribution is a controlled account of when agent repair destroys already-established properties under evaluator disagreement.

STRONGEST RESEARCH DIRECTION — INTERPRETATION, NOT AN ESTABLISHED NOVELTY CLAIM
Study security-preserving revision when an agent's evaluator conflicts with independently reproducible evidence. The distinct target would be the causal transition from a candidate that satisfies both a security property and legitimate functionality to a candidate that violates one of them, with continuation pressure and observability controlled. Existing neighboring papers separately cover misleading feedback, unnecessary action, verifier defects, and provenance; the proposed combination needs a final search before any priority claim.

A clean experiment begins from independently validated candidate states, including secure-and-functional, secure-but-overblocking, unsafe-but-functional and invalid-code states. Fork identical histories into truthful and false verdict arms. Hold wording, remaining budget, action availability and continuation instructions constant. Include a no-change action and measure its use without treating nonresponse or adapter failure as abstention. Intervene separately on whether agents see the actual failing witness, an independent contrary witness, or only a verdict label. Preserve unmodified raw records while rendering manipulated feedback to the agent.

The key hypothesis is conditional: independently checkable contrary evidence may reduce destructive revisions, but its benefit may depend on source attribution and the cost of another verification action. An equally informative alternative is that explicit continuation pressure dominates verdict truth, or that all forms of revision increase regression at equal rates. Such results would challenge a false-feedback-only narrative.

Track security and functionality at every candidate, not just final acceptance. Distinguish insecure acceptance, overblocking, syntax/runtime failure, missing verdict, unsupported completion, voluntary stop and controller stop. Security here must always mean the independently specified bounded properties actually tested, with residual coverage limitations stated.

SECONDARY OPPORTUNITIES — SPECULATIVE
1. Evidence retention versus evidence acquisition: test whether the agent needs a new check or merely a durable representation of an already verified invariant. Fixed receipt content with randomized position can separate memory/attention failure from trust failure. This is motivated by A33 and A23 but is not yet demonstrated in security repair.
2. Cross-attempt contamination: compare ephemeral feedback with persistent verbal-reflection memory. A false verdict might affect later tasks even after the current candidate is restored. A10 motivates the architecture, not the predicted harmful transfer.
3. Clarification as a third action: introduce an explicit request for a checker witness or human specification decision, rather than forcing edit versus stop. A49 already establishes user interaction as an evaluation target; the new question would concern the cost and security value of resolving inconsistent verification.
4. Availability-versus-integrity asymmetry: test whether evaluators and agents react differently to unsafe access and excessive denial. The manuscript's CSRF and key-rotation observations suggest this contrast, but task-family confounding currently prevents interpreting it as a discovered law.

ESSENTIAL BASELINES
A18 motivates a scripted localization/repair/validation baseline. A43 and A45 motivate independent sampling at matched compute. A11–A15 require distinguishing self-critique, tool-grounded correction and correction-trained models. A39 and A40 motivate generated/expanded test comparisons while warning that agreement is not ground truth. A17 makes the custom controller itself an experimental factor. A48 and A50 motivate joint utility/security reporting. The manuscript's fixed guide should be called static guidance, not retrieval-augmented generation; A29–A34 provide the actual retrieval comparators if a retrieval mechanism is introduced.
