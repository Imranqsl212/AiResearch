INTERPRETATION OF THE MANUSCRIPT
The historical hidden veto is a failure of experimental operationalization. Zero acceptance in the veto arm follows from the implementation. The nine final raw-test passes establish disagreement between layers of the evaluator, not nine secure programs. Missing action-level raw results prevent retrospective reconstruction of the desired transitions. Adapter-derived stopping labels cannot establish irrational persistence.

The finite continuation pilots are useful because candidate behavior is explicitly enumerable. They do not automatically measure realistic code repair, and repeated seeds of one authored transition do not establish mechanism diversity. The CSRF reject-all pattern suggests legitimate-use denial; key-rotation cases suggest a different direction. These are observed possibilities, not a general causal result. Treat independently implemented finite checkers as implementation cross-checks unless their specifications were independently reviewed.

The original headline 'misleading checking can produce bad repairs' is too close to established findings. Prior art is not fatal to the revised project, but an elaborate combination of known components is not inherently groundbreaking. The stronger contribution needs a new empirical boundary or mechanism, not a new name for verification.

PROPOSED OPPORTUNITIES, NOT FINDINGS
1. Evidence-sensitive preservation and recovery. Freeze a candidate plus public history before its verdict. Randomize the verdict and access to relevant independent evidence; include correct and defective candidate strata. Keep later checks truthful and stopping opportunities equal. Compare against retain, edit-after-failure, and verify-before-edit policies. The hypothesis is selective benefit: evidence reduces damage to correct states while preserving recovery from defects. Failure to beat a simple fixed policy would narrow the scientific claim.

2. Direction of damage as a causal mechanism. Cross misleading failure with tasks where reject-all is easy or costly. Measure unsafe acceptance and legitimate denial on separate held-out cases, plus their transitions over time. Obscure mode names and reproduce the effect in actual code. A directional effect that disappears under relabeling would identify interface sensitivity rather than a broad security-repair tendency.

3. Independent failure modes rather than checker count. Compare duplicated checks, different implementations of one specification, and genuinely complementary properties at matched cost. Hold out checker mutations during development. The hypothesis is that useful disagreement depends on distinct error coverage. Correlated mistakes are already known; the contribution would be a concrete measured decision boundary in interactive repair.

VALIDATION REQUIREMENTS
Use deletion/reject-all, no-change, superficial exploit suppression, genuine repair, and malformed candidates to audit the scoring pipeline before agent data. Record candidate hash, full source, task contract, tool version, raw result, acceptance policy, delivered message, and termination cause. Keep unresolved semantics explicit. A differential disagreement generates a question for adjudication, not its own ground truth. Exact match to a developer patch is neither necessary nor sufficient for correct behavior.

For real-code transfer, pair exploit and functional checks, preserve original project tests, add reviewed developer-intent properties, and manually adjudicate ambiguous disagreements. Separate checker development data from evaluation mutations. Static-analysis agreement and high coverage do not prove security. Fuzzing requires repeated runs and declared budgets; flaky tests require a recorded retry policy. Keep benchmark contamination separate from oracle validity.

REVIEW OF ROOT NARRATIVE
The draft already makes the core distinctions correctly. Recommended edits: soften 'High for the specific causal design' to conditional, uncertain originality; add Ahmed2026 as a close prior-art threat; explicitly say paired security/functionality validation is already present in PatchBench/PVBench/Vul4Py; identify Qi2015's deletion baseline as the analogue of reject-all. The promising result would be a validated causal mechanism that generalizes, with a meaningful null outcome. It is not yet established by the manuscript.
