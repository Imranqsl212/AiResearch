RESEARCH REVIEW — 16 NOVEMBER 2026

The report has all 11 requested sections and 150 annotated publications. bibliography.json supplies structured records; bibliography.bib is a conservative BibTeX transport export using misc/howpublished to preserve verified venue/status without inventing publication fields.

Publication identities, links, authors, years, and status were checked against primary sources. Reading depth is specified individually. Many entries are abstract-level analyses; closest novelty threats received targeted full-text review. This is a purposive interdisciplinary review, not a systematic review, meta-analysis, replication of cited findings, or claim of cover-to-cover reading of all 150 papers.

Source evidence notes are in sources/. review_manifest.json records manuscript/input hashes and the four successful read-only local checks. verification_report.json records corpus and report integrity checks. The original manuscript and experimental records were not edited; no new model experiments were run.
