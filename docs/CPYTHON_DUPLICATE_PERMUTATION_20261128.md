# Conditional uncertainty for the CPython duplicate-prompt comparison

This secondary calculation was specified after the 64-response collector started and before the authors of this calculation opened the current response choices. Its two endpoints and the cross-minus-within statistic follow the existing duplicate-prompt protocol. Include only histories with all four usable draws. Always report that denominator and the parent study's full-cohort missingness bounds. This analysis does not alter any assignments or primary grades.

Within each history the collector randomized four labeled decision positions, two for each display. Conditional on its four observed outcomes, enumerate all six ways to assign the no-display label twice and shared-display label twice. Under the sharp null that the label changes no potential response at any position, with no effects carried between positions, every assignment has equal weight. Equivalently, the calculation is a label-exchangeability diagnostic conditional on this particular panel. Add the within-history cross-minus-within scores in integer quarter units and convolve their distributions over the complete histories. Count assignments tying or exceeding the observed statistic for each of the two endpoints. A positive statistic is the alternative of interest because under independent stable draws its expectation is a squared distance. Report exact upper-tail fractions and Holm-adjusted values over these two secondary tests. A negative observed value remains negative. Inference for a population of new tasks or provider versions requires additional assumptions.
