The [historical directional-error audit](../analysis/c5_persistent_boolean_errors_20261123.json) covers the two persistent-cohort pairs with native Boolean allowance semantics. At baseline both onsets were correct. The truthful branches permitted one of seven prohibited cases at termination and the false-failure branches permitted five. Neither denied a required case. The audit records one reason for each of the twelve exclusions.

The [prospective endpoint reader](PROSPECTIVE_ENDPOINT_READER.md) replays raw observations before it derives its tables. Where a task exposes Boolean requirements, it separately counts acceptance of prohibited cases and denial of required cases. Other task representations keep their joint endpoint and an explicit missing value for the separate measurements.

The two coder directories are local and contain blank responses. Each reviewer receives only their own packet. Independent human decisions must precede any adjudication or agreement statistic. The proposed registry submission should bind the exact protocol and per-model schedule digests to immutable registration responses before eligible collection. Approved public and approved embargoed OSF registrations can both establish priority. [OSF registration guidance](https://help.osf.io/article/330-registrations)

The proposed 19-family schedule would use at most 13,680 generation requests per model, or 54,720 across four models. At the base text rates observed on 23 November, using every request and billing exactly 1,024 prompt and 256 completion tokens on each would cost approximately USD 168 across the four models. At 2,048 prompt tokens with the same completion count, the corresponding estimate is USD 241. These scenarios exclude other fees or distinct reasoning charges. Actual branching, token consumption, pricing and available routes determine the bill. No spend has been authorized.
